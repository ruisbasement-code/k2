K2 Climbing

You're at Base Camp on the Godwin-Austen Glacier, 5,000 m. The real mountain, from satellite elevation data.

Unroped, you can't go up anything steeper than 30°, and from 22° a slip becomes a real fall, worst on ice. Clip into the fixed ropes with C: you can still move around, and the rope gets you up steep ground, speeds you along it and catches you if you slip.

The air thins as you go: pace drops to under 100 m an hour near the summit, and your stamina drains faster and refills slower. Hard falls cost health; lose it all and you die.

The weather changes: clear days, cloud, high winds, fog, afternoon snowstorms and whiteouts. Fresh snow loads the slopes and avalanches come down; in a camp you hear the forecast on the radio.

The mountain clock runs 10× real time (T to change). Press 2 to jump to the foot of the ropes.
