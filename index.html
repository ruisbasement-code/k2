K2 Climbing

You're at Base Camp on the Godwin-Austen Glacier, 5,000 m. The real mountain, from satellite elevation data.

Below 30° you walk. Steeper, you climb, and over 45° an unroped slip sends you sliding, so clip into the fixed ropes with C. The air thins as you go: pace drops to under 100 m an hour near the summit, and your stamina drains faster and refills slower.

The mountain clock runs 10× real time (T to change). Press 2 to jump to the foot of the ropes.
